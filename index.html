The Signature Tomato Jam

Deliciously Sweet.
Softly Savory.

A rich, handcrafted fruit conserve unlike anything on the market. Lusciously sweet forward with a gentle, subtle hint of savory richness that makes every bite unforgettable.

Plump, vine-ripened tomatoes, a kiss of cane sugar, and a chorus of warm spices, slow-cooked until the kitchen smells like a late-summer evening. No shortcuts. Just pure, unadulterated nectar.

Thick enough to stand a spoon in. Handmade in Ontario.

The Dubai Chocolate

Not a bar. Not a lollipop.
A Nosh.

Every Nectar & Nosh bar is built for one moment: the snap. A shell that breaks clean, then gives way — crisp and creamy in the same bite.

Hold it. Bite it. Hear it go.

Because big chocolate bars are a dare — half gets eaten, half gets rewrapped, and everyone pretends that's normal. Ours is two fingers on a stick, sized to the exact width of a craving.

Contains pistachio and sesame.

Our Story

It began with a single, unforgettable bite.

At a twilight cocktail party, a beautifully crafted burger slider was served with an unexpected star: tomato jam. What started as one unforgettable bite became a hundred nights in one Ontario kitchen, getting every detail right.

Behind every jar and every bar is one maker: Danny C. Thirty years of building software, now building with his hands.
